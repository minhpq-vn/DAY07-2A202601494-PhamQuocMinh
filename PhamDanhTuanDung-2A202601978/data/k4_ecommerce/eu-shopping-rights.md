# Seller status and protection

Online marketplaces must indicate whether a seller is a trader or a private individual. EU consumer legislation protects purchases from traders; the marketplace must warn buyers when an offer comes from a private individual.

# Fourteen-day cooling-off period

For an order made online, by phone, or from a catalogue, the buyer generally has fourteen days after receiving the goods to cancel and return the order without giving a reason. Exceptions include tickets and bookings for specific dates, regular grocery deliveries, personalised goods, opened sealed media or software, digital content after downloading or streaming begins with consent, goods bought from a private person, and urgent repairs already agreed.

# Delivery and faulty goods

The trader is responsible for damage from dispatch until the buyer receives the goods. Faulty or misdescribed goods can be repaired or replaced free of charge; where neither is possible, the buyer can request a price reduction or refund. The buyer should first contact the trader who sold the product or service.
